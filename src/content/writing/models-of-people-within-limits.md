[General Social Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17407) caught my attention because it studies predicting people's choices within designed game families.

A model of behavior is useful only if we understand the setting it was tested in. Games give researchers defined choices and outcomes. Everyday decisions involve context that may never appear in that setup.

I find the work interesting as a way to study what transfers across related situations. I would want to know which people, incentives, and games the evaluation covers, and where its predictions fail.

I would not extend success in those experiments to arbitrary individual behavior. That would require separate evidence.

For me, the useful question is how we build a model that makes its limits as clear as its predictions.
