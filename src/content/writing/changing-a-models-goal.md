[Steering Language Model Goals with Value Transplant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34056) interests me because it tries to change a model's behavior through an internal intervention.

The experiments use specially trained coding models. That controlled setting helps investigate whether changing an internal representation changes the goal-directed behavior the researchers observe.

I find interventions useful because they can provide evidence beyond a correlation between an activation and an output. They still need careful controls and a clear account of what changed.

I would treat this as preliminary causal evidence within the tested setup. It does not establish a general method for aligning language models.

The next question I would ask is whether the effect survives different tasks, training setups, and ways of expressing the goal.
