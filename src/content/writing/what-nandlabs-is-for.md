NandLabs began as a place to build products. I still want it to be that. But “building” is larger than shipping an application.

It includes explaining why a system works, noticing where an organization fights its own architecture, keeping a useful link long enough to add context, and putting a business idea into words before mistaking enthusiasm for evidence.

This publication is a place for that work.

## Thinking should leave artifacts

Ideas improve when they have to survive contact with a reader. Writing makes the gaps visible: the undefined term, the convenient assumption, the conclusion that arrived before the argument.

I want to publish several kinds of artifacts here:

- technical essays about systems and engineering;
- shorter notes from work in progress;
- curated links with an explanation of why they matter; and
- business ideas framed as hypotheses, not prophecies.

The formats are deliberately mixed. Curiosity rarely arrives according to an editorial calendar.

## Evidence over theater

My professional work is part of this site, but it is not the whole site. I want to discuss the problems, constraints, and lessons behind the work—not reproduce a résumé in larger type.

The same standard applies to NandLabs experiments. A small product that teaches something true is more valuable than an ambitious story with no evidence behind it.

## An open invitation

The best outcome for this site is not a larger archive. It is a better conversation—with engineers, founders, customers, peers, and people working on problems I have not encountered yet.

That feels like a worthwhile experiment.
