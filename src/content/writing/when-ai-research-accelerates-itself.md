[CASP's report on a possible intelligence explosion](https://casp.ac/reports/intelligence-explosion) and its [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36054) examine a feedback loop: better AI could accelerate AI research, which could produce better AI.

I find the question interesting because faster execution of a research task is only one part of the loop. We also need useful experiments, reliable evaluation, access to compute, and results that improve the next system.

I would look for evidence that automated research produces validated advances across repeated cycles. I would also ask where the bottlenecks move as more of the work becomes automated.

The report is conditional risk and policy analysis. It is not evidence that an intelligence explosion has begun.

That distinction matters for oversight. We can decide which capabilities and research outcomes to monitor without pretending we already know how quickly the loop will develop.
